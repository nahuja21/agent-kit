================================================================================
Headline: $1.2 M savings across 14 indirect categories for PE-backed dermatology platform
Problem Statement: Rapid acquisitions left APDerm with fragmented purchasing, rising supplier costs, and no centralized procurement function.
Approach:
  • Performed detailed AP spend analysis to identify quick-hit and strategic categories
  • Executed two-phase sourcing roadmap balancing speed and change management
  • Leveraged Treya GPO contracts for commoditized categories and ran competitive RFPs for others
  • Collaborated closely with clinicians to preserve product quality and service levels
  • Implemented savings tracking and supplier compliance processes
Top Levers: GPO utilization, Competitive RFPs, Incumbent supplier negotiations, Spend consolidation, Contract benchmarking
Value Delivered: Treya unlocked nearly 20 % cost reduction without disrupting patient care, boosting enterprise value by over $12 million.
Unique Approach: Speed – delivered first-wave savings within 4 months, Data-driven benchmarks informed negotiations, Hands-on implementation support minimized client workload
